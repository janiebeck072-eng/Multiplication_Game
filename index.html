How to make a delivery

Break the multiplier into place values. Find each partial product, then combine them.

Example: 24 × 13
24 × 3 = 72
24 × 10 = 240
72 + 240 = 312

The 1 in 13 represents 10, so the second partial product is 240.

Earn 3 stars for a delivery with every step correct on the first try, 2 after retries, or 1 with a hint or revealed step. Every completed delivery counts!
